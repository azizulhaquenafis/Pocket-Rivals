# Pocket Rivals privacy and data handling

Draft for publication, 2026-10-13. Add your publisher identity, contact email, and effective release date before hosting this as the public privacy policy.

Pocket Rivals is an offline collection for solo play or two people sharing one device. It does not create accounts, collect personal information, send analytics or crash reports, track users, show advertising, offer purchases, or contact a backend. Game artwork, fonts, code and sound synthesis are packaged locally.

The app saves sound/music preferences, overall duel wins/draws, tournament plans and results, arcade high scores, and unfinished game checkpoints in local app storage. Board checkpoints contain legal move histories; physical games contain positions and clocks. These records never leave the device. Names, contact details, location and device identifiers are not collected. Native Android cloud backup is disabled. Use Settings → Reset the scoreboard to erase scores, or clear app data/uninstall to erase all preferences and local data.

In the browser version, a service worker stores the application files in the browser's cache as they are played, or through the explicit Save all games offline option. Browser storage/site-data controls can remove these files. If hosted later, the hosting provider may process ordinary request logs; review that hosting arrangement and update this policy before publication. There is currently no hosted public deployment.

No app permissions are requested. Google Play or Android may independently process data under their own policies; Pocket Rivals contains no SDK to receive or transmit that information.

The game offers no social messaging, uploads, user-generated content or external game links. Review the Play target-audience and family policy requirements when selecting the final audience; no regulatory certification is claimed here.

Suggested Data safety basis: no data collected or shared by this app as implemented. The publisher must verify the release binary, dependency set and any later integrations before submitting the final declaration.

Ultimate Golf stores four local course records under `pocket-rivals.ultimate-golf.v1`. No player names or reference-game score files are imported.
