# Gameplay refinements — 2026-11-14

- **Spike Attacks:** players now have equal-mass solid contact. Both ordinary moves happen before symmetric separation, so an idle player can be pushed and equally opposing drives resist. Cover/walls constrain each correction; idle targets move with displaced players instead of pulling them back. Contact resolves before piston damage, with no AI exemptions.
- **Hammer Hit:** the needle retains its full 180-degree timing sweep and original scoring. A 54-unit pointer plus tip stays within the tower sides; a recessed arc makes its travel readable.
- **Water Guess:** mobile local results face the round winner; ties preserve the current reader and desktop/AI remain upright. Reveal duration excludes the rotation. Inactive keypads are dimmed during filling, turning and AI response wait; accidental digit presses cannot skip a result. Percentage-only winner-colored reveals and integer/unique guesses remain intact.
- **Orbit Hop:** smaller tier-specific planets, longer diagonal transfers and visible finite gravity fields with smooth inward pull. Surface contact is still required to land. No gravity outside the visible halo, no teleport capture. Medium retains a curved guide; Hard/Impossible reduce assistance and increase timing demands. See ORBIT_REFINEMENT.md.
- **Color Switch:** an endless rising checkpoint journey through rings, triangles, shutters, squares and wave ribbons. Five-stage chapters change the environment and rotation direction; safe transitions preserve lives. Arrow keys/A/D steer and Space hops; touch presses hop and drag steers. Tiers visibly change lane width, speed, hazard thickness and guidance. Drawn segments and collisions share geometry. Wave colors sweep through the entire narrow lane and use continuous phases, avoiding a snap at full rotations.
- **Previews:** original vector illustrations replace Lumberjack, Fruit Duel, Color Switch and Pocket Climber artwork. No stock/raster dependencies.

Validation: 72 relevant unit tests pass, including 2,400 Orbit transfers, 60 Color Switch shape/color/tier route scenarios, three uninterrupted Color stages per tier, 100 seeded Spike AI matches, contact/resistance/wall bounds and needle sweep bounds. Production TypeScript/Vite build succeeds. Fifteen browser scenarios passed across the targeted sweep and rerun: local pushing/resistance, Hammer turns and AI delay, Orbit/Color controls and cleanup, full Water match, both device orientations, duplicate-answer rejection, AI wait, and all four production previews. The Water full-match test now waits for readable reveal time rather than a fixed delay that included rotation.
