# Gravity Runner

One shared portrait corridor; cross-track physics y=0..170, progress scrolls vertically. Both runners share distance, hazards and legal flips. Orange Space/arrows/lower touch half; Blue WASD/Enter/L/upper half. Three rounds total per match; each survival win awards 1 point, while simultaneous deaths or two survivors at the 120-second deadline draw that round with no replacement round (higher score after 3 rounds wins, ties draw). Reset clock and speed for each new round. Pause/intermission time is excluded. A three-second settle-in precedes steeper continuous speed acceleration from 145 toward a validated 450 maximum.

CourseStream owns bounded seeded endless generation. It chooses weighted map-specific motifs (including tight gauntlets, thread-the-needle squeezes, retracting crusher corridors and orbiting roamers) while excluding the previous two choices; there is no repeating pattern cycle. Widths, sides, counts, spacing and phases vary. Later sections are denser, with at least 180 units of clearance between independent hazards. One early rail teaches the transfer. Rail gaps begin/end 180 units inside the support; pinches/islands keep rail faces legally runnable while outer walls remain lethal. Retire geometry behind the camera; keep history capped at 16.

Map trap families: ice icicles/frost blocks/orbiting snowballs; green roots/brambles/orbiting spores; blue sinusoidal water jets/octagonal buoys/orbiting jellyfish; purple crystals/timed phase gates/orbiting void orbs. Maps.ts contains lightweight metadata/previews only. CourseStream and physics stay lazy. Do not add other games' logic here.

ObstacleGeometry is authoritative for rendering and collision: triangle/trapezoid/convex silhouettes use SAT against the 26×18 runner footprint; jets change depth with seeded time and phase gates actually open. Pass the same round age to gameplay, renderer and AI forecasts. Open phase gates warn before activation. Runnable platform faces use swept landing; front penetration and returning-wall rescue are rejected. 120 Hz integration, gravity 900, 160 ms legal flip lock. No runner-to-runner collision or AI exemptions.

AI forecasts use the same advanceRunner, age-dependent speed and moving trap phases. Difficulty controls reaction distribution, visible lookahead and one/two-flip search. Medium uses approximately .62s lookahead and .30s observations; Hard uses 1.45s/.085s and Impossible 1.75s/.04s. Safe movement remains stable. Seeded replay uses MatchRandom. Balance checks measure legal survival distributions; do not require Medium to solve every route or manufacture errors for stronger tiers.

GravityView interpolates presentation only. GravityArt/TrapArt retain one masked Graphics; no extra RAF/textures. Round numbers are removed from the rail. Dispose graphics and mask; host owns input cleanup.

Tests: gravity-runner.test.ts, gravity-variation.test.ts, expansion-framerate.test.ts, premium-motion.test.ts; gravity-landscapes.spec.ts, gravity-shared.spec.ts and expansion/tournament browser regressions. Preserve independent automatic registration and lazy loading.

Map choice is automatic on launch, ignoring stale variant selections. Each subsequent round chooses uniformly from the other three maps using the match seed and round number; draws also change maps. Geometry, speed, clock and presentation reset together. Only one match is constructed at initialization. Selected modules preload while setup is open; factories are not instantiated early. AI reuses its forecast buffer and skips forecasts before geometry is within reach.
Moving motifs combine along-track sliders, across-track movers and retracting side pistons on every map. Motion uses seeded phase/period and round age. obstacleBounds returns actual start/end and low/high; along-track motion stays inside the original x/width broad-phase envelope, preserving neighboring clearances. Rendering uses interpolated age and the exact collision silhouette, with slim tracks, sockets and amber pre-extension lights. AI forecasts use the identical motion functions. Keep 400 seeded legal route checks across four maps.
