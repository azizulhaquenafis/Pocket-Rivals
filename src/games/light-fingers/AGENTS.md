# Light Fingers

Rules owns waiting/signal/active/between/done and first-to-five. Signal becomes claimable only after the render update presents it. FirstResponse arbitrates the first tap per player; same-frame ties leave the gem available and lock both players briefly (1.25s). Early/wrong grabs impose a 2.5s cooldown with no score changes; a successful steal gives only its owner a point. Only an attempted failed grab triggers cooldown; an idle rival is not penalized. AI starts its calibrated delay only after the shared visible signal (minimum 120ms); no hidden activation access or premature response. Pause freezes model time.

AI identities use the lazy `shared/ai/MatchRandom.ts` utility; keep tactical parameters inside this module and preserve personality across rounds. See `docs/AI_REFINEMENT.md` for seeded replay and audit commands. Preserve legal actions and bounded worker/motion behavior when adding variety.

LightArt draws an atmospheric museum-vault floor with corner spotlight fixture, volumetric beam, floor light pool, dust motes, multi-faceted sparkling gem plinth, and two opposing sculpted thief gloves (with orange/blue cuffs, idle anticipation wiggle, curved snatch arcs, fist-clench gem carry, recoil shudder, and clean edge cooldown bars), clipped inside the board mask in both local and AI modes. HandMotion derives reach, recoil, grayscale recovery and carrying poses from model time; input cooldown deadlines determine when the hand returns fully colored to rest. Never add countdown text. A successful hand closes and carries the gem home; interrupted/failed animations must not restore a stolen gem. Round results tint the field by winner; keyboard instructions stay in setup.
