Fixed 120Hz steps, bounded object counts, no extra timers. Pause clears held gestures. Report finishArcade, never duel scores. Keep this module lazy and independently removable. Journey progression is endless with five-stage chapters: ring, triangle, shutters, square and wave. World-space checkpoints rise; a 700ms camera transition freezes input, momentum and collisions. Lanes shift between stages. Left/right arrows or A/D steer; touch drag steers and its press hops. All obstacles share the drawn segment geometry with circle collision, including visible outlines. Ring seams count the whole ball footprint. Wave colors must sweep fully through the narrowest lane and retain continuous phase across 2π; never wrap fractional-frequency animation clocks. Medium has wider lanes/slower shapes/crossing cues; Hard and Impossible narrow lanes, thicken hazards and increase speeds. Keep deterministic route checks for all five shapes and all four ball colors, plus uninterrupted three-stage sequences.
