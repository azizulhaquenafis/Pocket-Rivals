# Solo arcade module

ShooterWorld.ts owns fixed-step combat, swept segment collisions, damage immunity and bounded pools (80 projectiles, 8 pickups, 24 effects). Waves.ts defines formations, enemy types and difficulty progression. Powerups.ts owns gear metadata and rare-life eligibility. ShooterArt.ts draws original vectors; SpaceShooterGame.ts binds host inputs, HUD, audio and cleanup. Report finishArcade; never duel points.

Gold supply ships appear on odd levels and six-level life milestones; ordinary loot drops only every 32 kills. Catching capsules upgrades cannons or grants timed lasers, rapid fire, wingmates and shields. Advancing a level clears all equipment and uncollected equipment capsules; lives, score and falling life capsules persist. Same-level formation retries do not clear gear. Extra lives are milestone-only with a cooldown/cap; never add them to ordinary random loot. Preserve player/enemy projectile reservations and per-projectile piercing hit history. Use seeded random injection for tests. No additional render loops or timers.

Use only this module and shared contracts/helpers. Phaser owns the render loop; use fixed stepping and host-cleaned inputs. Relevant checks: tests/unit/star-patrol.test.ts, tests/unit/phase3.test.ts, tests/browser/star-patrol.spec.ts and tests/browser/phase3.spec.ts. Arcade metadata must exclude tournaments. See docs/STAR_PATROL.md for tuning and verification.
